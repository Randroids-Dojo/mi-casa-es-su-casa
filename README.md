# Mi Casa Es Su Casa

A browser-based life simulation where persistent little people live in dollhouse homes. Visit anyone's character by name — their house, their life, their story.

Inspired by the 1985 Commodore 64 game *Little Computer People*.

## Play

Visit `/<name>` to watch a character live their life. There are no accounts — the name is the key.
